Consent & Release

Ohio-oriented draft for attorney review. Each visit requires fresh consent. Read every section before signing.

1. Voluntary authorization

I voluntarily request the tattoo described on this form, at the placement recorded for this visit. I understand tattooing punctures the skin with needles to deposit pigment and creates a permanent alteration of appearance. I have had an opportunity to ask questions and may stop the procedure.

2. Procedure risks

Known risks include pain, bleeding, swelling, bruising, infection, allergic or inflammatory reaction, scarring, changes in pigmentation, uneven healing, pigment migration or fading, and unexpected cosmetic results. Even appropriate hygiene and care cannot eliminate every risk.

3. Health information and artist review

I confirm that the health answers I provided for this visit are accurate to the best of my knowledge. I understand the artist may postpone or decline the procedure when health concerns require review or documentation from a licensed physician. I understand that tattoo artists do not provide medical diagnoses.

4. Design and placement approval

Before the procedure begins, I will inspect the final design, lettering, spelling, size, orientation, and body placement and communicate any changes. I understand that tattoos are difficult and costly to alter or remove.

5. Healing and aftercare

I understand that healing varies and depends partly on aftercare and individual factors. I will follow the written and verbal aftercare instructions, monitor for complications, and seek medical attention when appropriate.

6. No guarantee of cosmetic result

I understand that exact color, healed appearance, symmetry, and pigment retention cannot be guaranteed, and that touch-ups may be needed.

7. Independent artists

I understand that an artist performing services at the studio may be an independent contractor rather than an employee. This disclosure does not eliminate duties or legal responsibilities that the studio or artist may independently owe.

8. Assumption of inherent risks

I knowingly accept the inherent risks of tattooing described above. This acknowledgment does not waive rights that cannot legally be waived.

9. Proposed release of ordinary-negligence claims — attorney review required

To the extent permitted by applicable Ohio law, I agree to release the studio, its owners, personnel, and the performing artist from claims for personal injury or property damage arising from ordinary negligence in connection with the requested tattoo procedure. This proposed release does not apply to gross negligence, reckless or intentional misconduct, or any other claim that cannot lawfully be released. The scope and enforceability of this provision must be approved by Ohio legal counsel before production use.

10. Accuracy and understanding

I have reviewed the information for this visit, have had the opportunity to ask questions, and understand that a new visit requires new health answers and a new signed consent. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder is intended to remain effective to the extent permitted by law.

This draft is not attorney-approved, is not a final release, and is not suitable for real clients.
